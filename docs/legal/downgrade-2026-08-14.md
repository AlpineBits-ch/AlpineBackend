# Downgrade and Data Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your content when a paid plan ends and a guild or an account returns to a lower tier. It forms part of the Venta Paid Services Terms and uses the same defined terms.

There is one commitment in this document and everything else is detail:

> **We never delete your content because you stopped paying.**
>
> When something no longer fits inside a lower tier's limits, we freeze its growth. We do not remove what is already there. Over the emoji limit means the newest emoji stop working, not that they are deleted. Over the storage limit means new uploads are blocked, not that files are removed. Your messages are never affected at all.

This is a commitment to you and not only a description of how the software currently behaves. We consider a change that would make a downgrade destructive to be a material change to these terms, requiring the notice in section 12.

## 1. When This Applies

This policy applies whenever the capacity available to a guild or an account is reduced, whichever of these caused it:

1. You cancelled a subscription and the period you paid for ended.
2. You changed to a lower plan.
3. A renewal payment did not succeed and the grace period in section 11 of the Paid Services Terms expired.
4. A promotional grant, trial or credit-funded period reached its end date.
5. Boosts applied to a guild fell below the threshold for its tier.
6. We changed what a plan includes, with the notice required by section 18 of the Paid Services Terms.

It applies the same way in all six cases. There is no worse treatment for a lapsed payment than for a deliberate downgrade.

## 2. The General Rule

**2.1 Nothing you created is deleted because a plan ended.**

**2.2** Where the amount of something exceeds the new tier's limit, the excess is **disabled, frozen or reduced in quality**, in a way that can be undone.

**2.3** Where a capability is not available on the new tier, it stops working. It does not destroy what it produced while it was working.

**2.4** We tell you before it happens. Before a downgrade takes effect we notify the account holder and, for a guild plan, the guild owner, stating which limits will be exceeded and what the effect will be.

**2.5** After a downgrade, the guild's settings screens continue to show what is over a limit and what to do about it.

## 3. Messages and Conversation History

**3.1 Message history is unlimited on every tier, including the free tier, and a downgrade does not touch it.** Retention is not a plan lever on Venta and will not become one.

**3.2** No message is deleted, hidden, truncated or made unsearchable because a plan ended.

**3.3** Attachments already posted in a conversation remain available for download. See section 4.

## 4. Files and Storage

**4.1** Each tier includes an amount of guild storage and a maximum upload size.

**4.2 If a guild is over its storage limit after a downgrade, existing files are kept and remain downloadable. New uploads to that guild are blocked until usage is below the limit.**

**4.3** Nothing is deleted by us to bring usage under the limit. Deleting files is a decision for the people in the guild, using the ordinary delete controls, and it frees space immediately.

**4.4** The maximum upload size applies to new uploads only. A file that was uploaded when a larger limit applied stays where it is and continues to work.

**4.5** Where both your own subscription and the guild's plan set an upload ceiling, the lower of the two applies.

## 5. Custom Emoji, Stickers and Sounds

**5.1 Custom emoji, stickers and sound effects over the new limit are disabled, not deleted.**

**5.2** Which ones are disabled is determined by when they were added: **the most recently added are disabled first**, until the number remaining is within the limit. Older ones keep working, which means the ones a community has been using for longest are the ones that survive.

**5.3** A disabled emoji still exists. It is visible in the guild's settings, marked as over the limit, its image is retained, and messages that already used it continue to display it.

**5.4** Deleting other emoji re-enables disabled ones automatically, oldest disabled first, up to the limit.

**5.5** Upgrading again re-enables all of them, exactly as they were.

## 6. Voice and Video

**6.1** Voice and video limits are reduced to the new tier's values: room size, resolution and frame rate ceilings, and the number of simultaneous video publishers.

**6.2 A room over the participant limit is not closed and nobody is ejected.** Quality is reduced instead, and the reason is shown to participants. This follows the general rule in the Paid Services Terms that we reduce quality rather than refuse an action.

**6.3** Voice sessions are live. There is no stored artifact of a call to lose, because we do not record calls.

## 7. Bots and Integrations

**7.1** Bots installed above the new tier's limit are **deactivated, not uninstalled**. Their configuration, permissions and any data they hold in the guild are kept.

**7.2** The most recently installed are deactivated first, on the same reasoning as section 5.2.

**7.3** Removing another bot, or upgrading again, reactivates them.

**7.4** A bot's own data lives with whoever operates that bot, not with us, and this policy cannot make commitments about it.

## 8. Guild Modules and Optional Features

**8.1** Where a guild module is not included in the new tier, the module is switched off. Its content is retained.

**8.2** For example, if forums are not included on the new tier, existing forum posts are not deleted. The forum becomes read-only or hidden, and it returns intact if the tier is restored.

**8.3** Switching a module off removes the permissions that only that module used. It never removes a permission that exists independently of the module.

**8.4 A downgrade never removes a guild owner's or administrator's ability to manage the guild**, including reaching the settings screens where a plan is bought, changed or cancelled. This is deliberate and is treated as a hard rule in the software, not as a configuration choice.

## 9. Vanity Invites and Custom Domains

**9.1** A vanity invite or custom domain not included in the new tier stops resolving.

**9.2** The name is held in reserve for your guild for **90 days**, so that restoring the plan restores the same address. After 90 days it is released and may be taken by somebody else.

**9.3** Ordinary invite links are unaffected on every tier.

## 10. Audit Log and Other Time-Windowed Records

**10.1** Some records are visible for a window that depends on the tier, most notably the guild audit log.

**10.2** On a downgrade, the visible window shortens. Entries outside the new window are hidden from the audit log view.

**10.3** Hidden entries are retained for the retention period stated in the Privacy Policy and are not deleted early by a downgrade. Restoring the plan restores the view of them, for as long as they still exist under that retention period.

## 11. What a Downgrade Never Affects

None of the following is reduced, disabled, hidden or delayed by any downgrade, on any tier:

1. **End-to-end encryption and key material.** Encryption is never a paid feature and is never weakened.
2. **Multi-factor authentication and every other account security control.**
3. **Your ability to export your data**, in full, in a machine-readable form, at any time.
4. **Your ability to delete your account or your content.**
5. **Moderation and safety tooling**, including bans, mutes, reports and the tools that support them.
6. **Access to the guild settings screens**, including the billing screens.
7. **Reading, sending and receiving messages**, and small-room audio voice.
8. **The content of any message, file, post or emoji that already exists.**

## 12. Reversing a Downgrade

**12.1** Restoring a plan restores everything that was disabled by the downgrade, automatically, to the state it was in.

**12.2** There is no re-upload, no reconfiguration and no support request needed.

**12.3** This is only true for as long as the underlying content still exists. Something you deleted yourself while downgraded is gone in the ordinary way, and something released under section 9.2 after 90 days may have been taken.

## 13. Deleting Your Account or Your Guild

**13.1** Deleting your account, or deleting a guild, is a decision you make and it does delete content. That is its purpose, and it is unrelated to billing.

**13.2** Account deletion is described in the Privacy Policy, including the 30 day grace period during which it can be undone.

**13.3** Cancelling a subscription is not a request to delete anything, and we never treat it as one.

## 14. When We End a Service for Cause

**14.1** Where we suspend or terminate a paid service for a breach under section 15 of the Paid Services Terms, the capacity reduction follows this policy in the same way.

**14.2** Content may separately be removed where it breaches the End User License Agreement or the law, or where a competent authority requires it. That is a moderation action with its own reasons and its own appeal route, and it is not a consequence of a plan ending.

## 15. Changes to This Policy

**15.1** We may change this policy. A change that would make a downgrade destructive to existing content is a material change and requires at least 30 days' notice by email and in the application, per section 18 of the Paid Services Terms.

**15.2** A new version is published as a new document with its own effective date. The previous version stays available.
