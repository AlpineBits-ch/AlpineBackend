## 2. Data We Collect

**Account data**: When you register, we collect your username, email address, and a hashed representation of your password. We never store passwords in plaintext. A profile picture is optional. If you provide a date of birth, we retain it to apply age-appropriate protections to your account.

**Message content**: Messages you send are stored on our servers so that they remain available in your conversation history across your devices. Private messages and direct calls are end-to-end encrypted, meaning we store the encrypted payload and cannot read its contents. See section 4.

**Message metadata**: We store metadata necessary to route, deliver and display messages: sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, and delivery and read status.

**Call data**: WebRTC calls are established peer-to-peer where possible. We retain call metadata (participants, start time, duration) but do not record or store call audio or video.

**Guild and community data**: Content posted in guilds (channels, messages, reactions) is stored on our servers to enable community features. Guild messages are not end-to-end encrypted between all members by default; they are encrypted in transit and at rest on our infrastructure.

**Technical and device data**: We collect information about your device and operating system version, app version, IP address, and connection data. This is used to maintain compatibility, diagnose issues, and ensure platform stability.

**Security records**: We keep an append-only record of security-sensitive events on your account - for example sign-ins, device registrations and removals, changes to your security settings, and access to your encrypted backups. These records exist so that unauthorised access to your account can be detected and investigated, including by you.

**Crash and error reports**: If the application crashes or encounters an error, we may collect a crash report containing technical diagnostics. These reports do not include message content. Where you have not consented to diagnostic data collection, these reports are pseudonymised and stripped of identifying fields.

**Download data**: When you download Venta from venta.gg, our infrastructure logs the request including IP address and timestamp for security and abuse prevention.

**Billing and payment data**: If you buy a paid service, we store your subscription and plan state, your billing history, an identifier for your customer record at our payment processor, and a reference to your payment method together with its brand, expiry and last four digits. **We never receive or store your full card number.** See section 6.

**Fraud-prevention identifiers**: If you redeem a trial, a promotional offer or promotional credit, we store non-reversible identifiers derived from your verified phone number, your device, and, where a payment method was required, the payment instrument. **These are hashes. The phone number itself is not stored for this purpose.** Section 6 explains this in full, because it is the part of this policy most likely to raise a question.

## 4. End-to-End Encryption

Private messages and direct calls between users are protected by end-to-end encryption using the MLS (Message Layer Security) protocol. This means that the content of your private communications is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers, and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient. AlpineBits KLG does not have the technical ability to read the content of end-to-end encrypted messages or calls.

We do, however, store the encrypted payload for as long as the message exists in your conversation history, so that your messages remain available to you across devices. Encryption protects the *content* from us; it does not mean the message is discarded after delivery.

End-to-end encryption does not conceal metadata. We can still see who communicates with whom, and when.

Guild channel messages are encrypted in transit and at rest on our servers, but are not end-to-end encrypted across all members by default. We are working toward expanding E2EE coverage in future releases.

## 6. Payments, Promotions and Fraud Prevention

This section applies only if you buy a paid service, or redeem a trial, a promotional offer or promotional credit. If you do neither, none of it applies to you, and nothing described here is collected.

### 6.1 Payments

Payments on venta.gg are processed by **Stripe**. When you pay, your card details are entered into a form supplied by Stripe and go directly to Stripe. They do not pass through our servers and we never hold your full card number.

What we hold is your subscription and plan state, your billing history, the identifier of your customer record at Stripe, and a reference to your payment method with its brand, expiry month and last four digits, so that you can recognise it in your billing settings.

Stripe acts as our processor for taking the payment, under a data processing agreement, and separately as its own controller for the fraud, sanctions and financial-regulation obligations that apply to it as a payment institution. Its own privacy notice governs that part.

To charge the correct rate of value added tax we also record the country associated with you and the evidence for it, and, if you give one, your VAT identification number. Where the law requires it, invoices and the records behind them are retained for the statutory period, which is longer than the retention described in section 7 for everything else.

The legal basis for all of the above is **performance of a contract** with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Art. 31(2)(a) nFADP) for taking the payment, and **compliance with a legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) for tax and accounting records.

### 6.2 One-per-account offers, and why they need identifiers

Free trials, promotional credit and similar offers are limited to one per account, and in some cases one per guild. Those limits are what make the offers possible to give away at all.

An account is nearly free to create, and so is a guild. A limit that is enforced only against the account it was granted to is not a limit: a new account claims the offer again, and does so indefinitely. The same applies to a limit that is forgotten once the offer expires. So the limit has to be tied to something harder to mint than an account, and the record of it has to outlive the offer.

### 6.3 What we store for this purpose

When you redeem an offer that is limited in this way, we store on the redemption record:

1. A **salted, non-reversible hash of your verified phone number**.
2. A **salted, non-reversible hash of the identifier of the device** used to redeem.
3. Where the offer required a payment method, a **hash of the fingerprint** that our payment processor returns for that payment instrument. That fingerprint is a stable value the processor derives for a given physical card; it is not the card number and cannot be turned into one.

Each hash is computed with a secret salt that we hold separately from the data. **We do not store the phone number, the device identifier or the card number for this purpose, and the stored hashes cannot be reversed to recover them.** They can only be compared with the hash of a value presented later.

Your phone number is separately stored against your account where you have verified one, for account security and recovery. That is a different purpose, described elsewhere in this policy, and the two are not linked through this record: what sits on a redemption is the hash and nothing else.

### 6.4 What we do with it

Exactly one thing: when a new redemption is attempted, we compute the same hashes from the values presented and compare them with the ones already stored. If they match a redemption that already exists, the offer is refused as already claimed.

We do not use these identifiers to build a profile, to link accounts for any other purpose, to target anything at you, or to make any decision about you other than whether a promotional offer may be redeemed again.

A refused promotional offer has no effect on your account, your access to Venta, or anything you have already paid for. It is not an automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects, and if you think a refusal is wrong you can ask a person to look at it by writing to legal@venta.gg.

### 6.5 Legal basis

The legal basis for the processing in 6.2 to 6.4 is our **legitimate interest** in preventing fraud and abuse of promotional offers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 31(1) nFADP). Fraud prevention is expressly recognised as a legitimate interest in Recital 47 GDPR.

We consider this proportionate because the data is limited to non-reversible hashes of identifiers you have already given us for other purposes, no new information is collected from you, the comparison is the only use, and the alternative is either not offering trials and promotional credit at all, or offering them in a form that funds itself out of everyone else's subscription.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. See section 8. Where you object and we uphold the objection, we delete the hashes; the consequence is that we may decline to offer you promotions that depend on this check.

### 6.6 How long we keep it

**Redemption records, including these hashes, are kept for as long as the offer they relate to is still limited, which in practice means permanently.** Expiry of an offer sets a field on the record; it does not delete the record. A record deleted when the offer expired would simply reset the limit, which is the abuse this exists to prevent.

We keep no other copy of these hashes and they are not exported to any third party.

### 6.7 Self-hosted instances

None of this section applies to a self-hosted Venta instance. Self-hosted instances have no billing, no promotions and no payment processor, and this processing does not exist there.

## 7. Data Retention

**Account and message data.** We retain your account data, and the messages in your conversations, for as long as your account is active. Messages are not deleted automatically after delivery - they remain available in your history until you or another participant deletes them, or until an account is deleted.

**Your own retention controls.** You can set your account to automatically delete the messages you have sent after a period you choose. This is off by default and applies only to your own messages.

**Connection and session records.** IP address and device information attached to a sign-in session are removed after 90 days. Records of ended sessions are deleted after 180 days.

**Security records.** The append-only security records described in section 2 are retained for the lifetime of the account, because their purpose is to make past unauthorised access detectable. IP addresses attached to them are removed after 180 days.

**Billing records.** Invoices and the records supporting them are retained for the period required by Swiss accounting and tax law, currently ten years, and are not deleted earlier on request. Subscription state that is no longer needed for that purpose is deleted with the account.

**Fraud-prevention identifiers.** The hashes described in section 6.3 are retained permanently, for the reason given in section 6.6.

**Data exports.** An export you request is available to download for 7 days, after which the archive is deleted.

**Account deletion.** When you request deletion, your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel and recover it. Sign-in is blocked immediately. After the grace period ends, we erase or anonymise your personal data across our services. Some records are retained in anonymised form where they are referenced by other users' data - for example, a message you sent in a shared conversation will show as coming from a deleted user rather than disappearing from their history. Where we are required to retain data for legal or compliance purposes, we retain only what is required, which includes the billing records above and the fraud-prevention hashes in section 6.3, neither of which identifies you once the account is gone.

## 10. Children

Venta is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at legal@venta.gg and we will delete it promptly.

Where an account's recorded date of birth indicates the account holder is a minor, we apply additional protections automatically: personalisation is disabled, the account cannot be found by email address or phone number, and who may contact the account holder directly is restricted. These protections cannot be switched off while the account holder is a minor.

## 11. Links, Previews and Embedded Players

**Link previews.** When a link is posted in a conversation, we fetch the linked page from our own servers to build a preview card showing its title, description and image. Any preview image is copied to our own storage and served from our own domain. This means the linked website is contacted by us, once, and not by each person who reads the message: it does not receive your IP address, your device details, or any indication that you saw the link. We store the resulting preview and the copied image alongside the message. Previews are not generated for end-to-end encrypted conversations, because we cannot read those messages.

**Embedded players.** Links to a fixed list of services - YouTube, Spotify, Vimeo and Twitch - can be played inside Venta. **Nothing is loaded from those services until you press play.** When you do, that service's player is loaded into the page and the service then receives your IP address, your browser and device details, and information about what you played. It may also set its own cookies and similar storage on your device. If you are signed in to that service, it may associate that activity with your account there.

This is a transfer of data to a third party that we do not control and do not receive a copy of. It happens only on your deliberate action, each time, and the legal basis is your consent given by that action. The YouTube player is loaded from a privacy-enhanced address that does not set Google's advertising cookies. Each service's own privacy and cookie policy governs what it does with the data; those are linked from our Cookie Policy.

If you never press play, none of this occurs. Your ability to read, send and receive messages does not depend on it.
