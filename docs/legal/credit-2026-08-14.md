# Promotional Credit Terms

Last updated: August 14, 2026

These terms govern promotional credit on Venta (venta.gg), operated by AlpineBits KLG ("we", "us", "our"). They form part of the Venta Paid Services Terms and use the same defined terms.

Promotional credit is a marketing benefit that we give away. **It is not money, it cannot be bought, and it cannot be turned back into money.** Everything below follows from that.

## 1. What Promotional Credit Is

**1.1** Promotional credit ("credit") is a promotional benefit, denominated in points, that we may issue to an account at our discretion. It can be redeemed for certain benefits on Venta, as described in section 9.

**1.2** Credit is held in a wallet attached to your account. Guilds do not hold credit.

**1.3** Credit is a limited, personal, revocable, non-transferable permission to redeem those benefits, on these terms. **It is not property, not a deposit, not a balance of funds, not a security, and not a claim to money.**

## 2. Credit Is Issued, Never Sold

**2.1 We give credit away. It is not for sale, and there is no way to buy it.**

**2.2** There is no route, product, offer, arrangement or combination of steps by which money you pay becomes credit in your wallet. If you ever appear to be offered one, it is not genuine; tell us at billing@venta.gg.

**2.3** No payment you make to us is consideration for credit. Credit accompanying a purchase is a gift alongside it and forms no part of what you paid for.

**2.4** Credit is never issued in settlement of a debt owed to you.

## 3. No Cash Value

**3.1 Credit has no cash value.**

**3.2** Points are a unit of account internal to Venta. They are not a currency, are not pegged to one, and confer no entitlement to any amount of money.

**3.3** Any cash figure shown next to a benefit is the price of that benefit in money for someone paying money. It is not the value of your credit and does not represent an exchange rate.

## 4. Not Refundable, Not Withdrawable, Not Convertible

**4.1 Credit is not refundable.** Nothing was paid for it, so there is nothing to refund.

**4.2 Credit cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, redeemed for money, or paid to you or anyone else in any form.**

**4.3 Credit cannot be converted into money, into a voucher, into a discount on a cash purchase, into a credit against an invoice, or into anything else of monetary value outside Venta.**

**4.4** No sequence or combination of actions may be used to achieve indirectly what 4.1 to 4.3 prohibit directly. Where an action or a series of actions would have that effect, we may reverse it.

**4.5** A benefit obtained with credit is not refundable in money. Where we reverse such a benefit for our own reasons, we return the credit that was spent, subject to its original expiry date. Expiry is never extended by a reversal.

## 5. Not Transferable

**5.1 Credit is not transferable.** It cannot be given, sold, traded, lent, assigned, gifted, pooled, bequeathed or otherwise passed to another account or another person.

**5.2** Credit does not survive the closure of the account it belongs to and is not part of any estate.

**5.3** Credit cannot be sold or offered for sale outside Venta. Doing so is a breach of these terms and of the End User License Agreement, and we may void the credit and suspend the account.

**5.4** Spending your own credit on a benefit that is applied to a guild you belong to is not a transfer. The credit is spent by you, from your wallet, at your direction.

## 6. Credit Expires

**6.1 Credit expires.** Each issuance is a separate lot with its own expiry date, set when it is issued.

**6.2** Unless a shorter or longer period is stated at the time of issue, a lot expires **12 months** after it is issued.

**6.3** The expiry date of every lot you hold is shown in your wallet from the moment it is issued. Nothing about expiry is hidden or discovered late.

**6.4** We notify you at least **30 days** before a lot expires, by email to the address on your account and in the application.

**6.5** When a lot expires, that credit is gone. It is not restored, extended or compensated. Expiry does not entitle you to anything.

**6.6** Expiry is deliberate. It keeps stockpiled promotional credit from becoming an open-ended liability and bounds what an abused promotion is worth.

## 7. Credit Is Not a Payment Instrument

**7.1** Credit is not electronic money, not a prepaid instrument, not a gift card, not a stored-value product, not a voucher redeemable for cash, and not a financial product of any kind.

**7.2** We do not hold funds for you. There is no float, no deposit and no account balance of money behind a wallet.

**7.3** The characteristics in sections 2 to 6 are what make that true, and we will not waive any of them for any account, for any reason.

## 8. How Credit Is Issued

**8.1** We may issue credit at our discretion, for example: as compensation after a confirmed service incident, as a referral reward to either or both sides of a referral, as goodwill in support of a complaint, as an allowance to try a new usage-based feature, or as part of a campaign or offer.

**8.2** Issuing credit once creates no entitlement to be issued it again, and no entitlement for anyone else to be issued it.

**8.3** Where credit is issued under a campaign, that campaign's own conditions apply alongside these terms, including who is eligible, how much, how often and by when it must be used.

**8.4** Eligibility for credit-issuing offers may require a verified phone number, a minimum account age, or other checks. Records of a redemption are kept permanently, including after the credit expires, so that a one-per-account offer means one per account. The fraud-prevention data behind those checks is described in the Privacy Policy.

**8.5** We may refuse to issue credit, and may reverse credit issued, where an offer was obtained through additional accounts, false information, automation, or any other circumvention of an eligibility limit.

## 9. What Credit Can Be Used For

**9.1** Credit can be redeemed for time-limited benefits offered in the credit catalogue in the application. What is offered changes; the catalogue in the application at the time you spend is the authoritative list.

**9.2** Typical benefits are a period of a paid plan on a guild, a period of Venta Plus on your own account, a boost applied to a guild you belong to, a temporary capacity increase for an event, or an allowance on a usage-based add-on.

**9.3 Every benefit that can be obtained with credit also has a plain price in money.** Credit is never the only way to obtain anything on Venta. If a benefit is offered only for credit, that is an error; tell us and we will correct it.

**9.4** Credit cannot be redeemed for anything that would outlast the period it paid for. It buys time-limited capacity, not permanent additions such as extra emoji slots, a vanity address or additional storage.

**9.5** Credit cannot be used to pay an invoice, to settle a subscription charge, or to offset an amount you owe us in money.

**9.6** A benefit obtained with credit is a promotional grant. It creates no invoice, is not a sale, and is not a purchase for the purposes of the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## 10. Points, and What They Are Worth

**10.1** Credit is denominated in points. The number of points a benefit costs is shown before you confirm.

**10.2** The cash price of the same benefit is shown alongside the point price, so that you can see what you are getting without having to guess.

**10.3** Showing the two prices together is not an exchange rate, does not value your points, and creates no right to receive the cash amount instead.

**10.4** We may change the point price of a benefit. A change never applies to a redemption already completed.

## 11. How Spending Works

**11.1** Spending consumes your **earliest-expiring credit first**. This is automatic and cannot be overridden, and it exists so that credit is used before it lapses rather than after.

**11.2** You cannot spend more credit than you hold. A balance can never go below zero.

**11.3** A benefit bought with credit for a period during which the same benefit is already active **starts when the current period ends**. Periods queue; they do not run concurrently and they are not lost.

**11.4** A redemption takes effect when it is confirmed. Confirming is what spends the credit.

**11.5** A redemption that fails is not charged. Where credit was deducted and the benefit was not granted, the deduction is reversed in full, with its original expiry date.

## 12. Your Balance and Your Ledger

**12.1** Your wallet shows your current balance, each lot, and each lot's expiry date.

**12.2** Every issue, every spend, every expiry and every adjustment is shown in a ledger you can read, in plain language, with dates.

**12.3** The ledger is the record of what happened to your credit. If it disagrees with what you expected, write to billing@venta.gg and we will explain the entries.

## 13. Limits

**13.1** We may set a maximum balance that a single wallet may hold. Credit that would take you above it may not be issued.

**13.2** We may set limits on how much credit may be issued under a campaign in total, and on how much any one account may receive from it.

**13.3** We may limit how much credit may be spent in a period, and on what.

**13.4** These limits exist to keep automated issuance from becoming an unbounded liability and to make farming a promotion unprofitable.

## 14. Reversal, Suspension and Forfeiture

**14.1** We may reverse, suspend or void credit, and cancel a benefit obtained with it, where:

1. it was issued in error, including a duplicate issue or a technical fault;
2. it was obtained through fraud, additional accounts, false information, automation, or circumvention of an eligibility limit;
3. it was obtained or used in breach of these terms, the Paid Services Terms or the End User License Agreement;
4. the account is suspended or terminated for fraud; or
5. we are required to do so by law or by a competent authority.

**14.2** Where an account is banned for fraud, outstanding credit is voided. The ledger entries remain, so that what happened stays auditable.

**14.3** Voiding credit entitles you to no compensation, since credit has no cash value and was not paid for.

**14.4** Where we act under 14.1 and you believe it was wrong, write to billing@venta.gg. We will review it and explain the outcome.

**14.5** Closing your account voids any credit in it. Credit is not paid out on closure, in any form. See section 5.2.

## 15. Errors

**15.1** Credit shown in your wallet in error, or issued in an amount other than the one intended, is not yours to keep, and we may correct it.

**15.2** Where the error was ours and you had already spent the credit in good faith before we corrected it, we will not claw back the benefit you received.

## 16. Discontinuing Credit

**16.1** We may discontinue promotional credit entirely.

**16.2** If we do, we will give you at least **90 days' notice**, during which any unexpired credit you hold can still be spent on the catalogue as it stands.

**16.3** Credit unspent at the end of that period lapses, with no entitlement to compensation, since it had no cash value at any point.

## 17. Tax

**17.1** Credit is a promotional benefit, not income paid to you, and we do not report it as such.

**17.2** Where receiving a promotional benefit has any tax consequence for you, that is your responsibility. This is not tax advice.

## 18. Self-Hosted Instances

Promotional credit exists only on venta.gg. A self-hosted Venta instance has no wallets, no credit and no billing of any kind, and nothing in this document applies to it.

## 19. Changes to These Terms

**19.1** We may change these terms. For a change that materially affects credit you already hold, we give at least 30 days' notice by email to the address on your account and in the application.

**19.2** The characteristics in sections 2 to 7 will not be changed in your favour on request, and are not subject to individual negotiation.

**19.3** A new version is published as a new document with its own effective date. The previous version stays available.
