# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This policy explains how to cancel a paid service on Venta (venta.gg), what happens when you do, and when you are entitled to money back. It forms part of the Venta Paid Services Terms and uses the same defined terms.

Two things are worth saying at the top, because they are the questions people actually have.

**Cancelling never deletes anything.** Cancellation ends a payment, not an account and not a guild. What happens to content that no longer fits inside a free-tier limit is set out in the Downgrade and Data Policy, and the short version is that growth is frozen and nothing is deleted.

**If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have a statutory right of withdrawal, and nothing in this policy takes it away.** Section 4 explains it, including the one thing that limits it: asking for immediate access.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to paid services bought from AlpineBits KLG on venta.gg: guild plans, Venta Plus, boosts and add-ons. It does not apply to purchases made through an app store, which are covered by section 16, and it does not apply to instances of Venta operated by anybody other than AlpineBits KLG.

## 2. How to Cancel

**2.1** Cancel in your billing settings in the Venta application, at any time, without giving a reason and without contacting us.

**2.2** You may also cancel by writing to billing@venta.gg from the email address on your account. Cancellation by email takes effect from the day we receive it.

**2.3** We do not require a phone call, a retention conversation, or a notice period to cancel.

## 3. What Cancellation Does

**3.1 Cancellation stops the next renewal.** It does not end the period you have already paid for.

**3.2** You keep the paid capacity until the end of the current billing period. The date is shown in your billing settings when you cancel.

**3.3** At the end of that period, the subject returns to the free tier, and the **Downgrade and Data Policy** applies. Content over a free-tier limit is frozen, disabled or reduced in quality. **It is not deleted.**

**3.4** You can resume a cancelled subscription before the period ends, and nothing will have changed.

**3.5** Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account, your messages, your guild or your files. Deleting an account is a separate action, described in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Statutory Right of Withdrawal: EU and EEA Consumers

**4.1** If you are a consumer resident in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you have the right to withdraw from a contract for a paid service **within 14 days** of entering into it, without giving a reason.

**4.2** The withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the contract was concluded, which for a subscription is the day you completed the purchase.

**4.3** Each renewal of a subscription is a continuation of the same contract, not a new one, so the 14 day period runs from the original purchase and not from each renewal. Where local law treats a renewal as a new contract, we apply the more favourable reading to you.

**4.4 Where you asked for the service to start immediately**, which is what buying access on Venta means, the following applies:

- For a **service** supplied over the billing period, you may still withdraw within the 14 days, and we may charge you an amount proportionate to what was supplied up to the moment you told us you were withdrawing. The rest is refunded.
- For **digital content supplied immediately** and not on a physical medium, the right of withdrawal is lost once supply has begun, **provided** you gave your express prior consent to that and acknowledged that you would lose the right. Section 6 describes exactly how and when that consent is taken.

**4.5** You do not lose any right of withdrawal because you used the service. Using it is what triggers the proportionate charge in 4.4, and nothing else.

## 5. Statutory Right to Cancel: UK Consumers

**5.1** If you are a consumer resident in the United Kingdom, you have an equivalent right to cancel within **14 days** of entering into the contract, under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

**5.2** The same treatment of immediate supply applies: for a service you may be charged proportionately for what was supplied before you cancelled, and for digital content supplied immediately with your express consent and acknowledgement, the right to cancel is lost once supply begins.

**5.3** Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 in respect of a service that is not carried out with reasonable care and skill, or digital content that is not of satisfactory quality, are separate from this and are not affected by anything in this policy.

## 6. Immediate Access and What You Agree To

**6.1** Paid capacity on Venta becomes available as soon as the payment succeeds. That is what people buying it want, and it is why sections 4 and 5 need this section.

**6.2** At checkout, before you confirm, you are asked to confirm two things separately and explicitly:

1. that you **request that we begin supplying the service immediately**, within the withdrawal period; and
2. that you **acknowledge** that, once supply has begun, you lose the right of withdrawal in respect of any digital content fully supplied, and that for the service element you will owe a proportionate amount for what was supplied if you withdraw.

**6.3** We keep a record that you gave these confirmations, when, and against which version of this policy.

**6.4** If you do not want immediate access, do not confirm it, and tell us at billing@venta.gg. The purchase will start at the end of the withdrawal period instead, with the full right of withdrawal intact until then.

## 7. How to Withdraw

**7.1** To withdraw, tell us clearly, in any unambiguous way, before the 14 days are up. Email billing@venta.gg, or use the form in section 8. Cancelling the subscription in your billing settings is also enough, provided you do it within the 14 days and it is clear that you are withdrawing rather than declining renewal; if there is any doubt, write to us.

**7.2** The deadline is met if you send your communication before the 14 day period expires.

**7.3** We will confirm receipt without delay.

## 9. Our Voluntary Refund Window

Independently of any statutory right, and in addition to it:

**9.1** If you bought a subscription or an add-on and have not meaningfully used what it unlocked, we will refund it in full **within 14 days of the charge**, on request to billing@venta.gg.

**9.2** This applies to renewals as well as to first purchases. A renewal you did not intend, noticed within 14 days and did not use is refunded.

**9.3** After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion. We will normally refund where a charge was clearly unintended or where something did not work as described.

**9.4** This voluntary window does not limit your statutory rights and does not replace them. Where both apply, you get whichever is better for you.

## 10. Faults and Statutory Guarantees

**10.1** If a paid service does not work as described, tell us at billing@venta.gg. We will try to fix it.

**10.2** If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a reduction in price or to a refund, in accordance with the consumer law that applies to you. For consumers in the EU and the EEA this includes the rights under the Digital Content Directive; for consumers in the UK it includes the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

**10.3** Nothing in this policy or in the Paid Services Terms excludes or limits any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## 11. What Is Not Refundable

Subject always to your statutory rights and to section 10:

1. **Promotional credit**, and anything bought with it. See section 12 and the Promotional Credit Terms.
2. **Boosts that have already been applied to a guild** for a period that has begun. A boost applied at your direction is consumed, in the same way as any other supplied service, and section 4.4 governs what happens if you withdraw within the statutory period.
3. **Usage-based add-on charges for usage that has already occurred.** We cannot un-transmit a recorded meeting.
4. **Amounts already refunded, charged back or reversed.**
5. Purchases where an eligibility limit was circumvented, an offer was obtained by false information, or the account is suspended for fraud.

## 12. Promotional Credit

**12.1** Promotional credit is issued by us and is never sold. There is no way to buy it, so there is no payment to refund.

**12.2** Credit has no cash value and is never converted into money, refunded, withdrawn, or paid out, in whole or in part, by any route or combination of routes.

**12.3** A purchase made with credit is not refundable in money, because no money was paid.

**12.4** Where we reverse a purchase made with credit for our own reasons, we return the credit, subject to its original expiry date. Expiry is not extended by a reversal.

**12.5** The full terms are in the **Promotional Credit Terms**.

## 13. Free Trials

**13.1** A free trial costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund while it is running.

**13.2** Cancel before the trial's stated end date and you are not charged. The end date is shown when the trial starts and in your billing settings throughout.

**13.3** If a trial converts to a paid subscription and you did not intend it to, section 9 applies: tell us within 14 days and we refund it.

## 14. Refund Method and Timing

**14.1** A refund is made to the payment method used for the original payment, unless that method is no longer available, in which case we agree another route with you.

**14.2** We issue refunds without undue delay and in any event within **14 days** of accepting the refund or receiving your withdrawal notice.

**14.3** How long the money takes to appear after that is your bank's or card issuer's business, not ours, and is commonly a further few working days.

**14.4** A refund carries no fee. Currency conversion differences between the original charge and the refund are set by your payment provider and are outside our control.

## 15. Chargebacks

**15.1** If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us first at billing@venta.gg. Almost every disputed charge on Venta is a renewal somebody forgot about, and we refund those under section 9 without argument.

**15.2** A chargeback raised without contacting us costs both of us more than a refund would have and is slower for you.

**15.3** We may suspend paid services on an account with an unresolved chargeback until it is resolved. We do not delete content because of a chargeback, and we do not suspend the account itself except where the dispute indicates fraud.

## 16. Purchases Made Through an App Store

Where a subscription was bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, that store is the merchant of record. **We cannot issue a refund for it and we cannot cancel it for you.** Cancellation is done in the store's own subscription settings and refund requests go to the store, under its own refund policy and its own timescales. Your statutory rights as a consumer against the store are unaffected. If a store declines a refund in circumstances where we would have granted one, contact us and we will do what we can within our own control, which may include promotional credit.

## 17. Consumers Outside the EU, EEA and UK

Sections 4 and 5 describe rights that come from EU, EEA and UK consumer law. If you are resident elsewhere, those particular rights may not apply to you, but the voluntary refund window in section 9 does, as do any mandatory consumer rights of your own country of residence.

## 18. Dispute Resolution

**18.1** If you are unhappy with how a refund request was handled, write to billing@venta.gg and ask for it to be reviewed. Tell us what outcome you want.

**18.2** Consumers may also be entitled to refer a dispute to an alternative dispute resolution body in their own country. We are not obliged to participate in, and do not commit to, arbitration before a consumer arbitration board.

**18.3** Section 19 of the Paid Services Terms governs applicable law and the courts, including the protection of the mandatory consumer rules of your country of residence.
