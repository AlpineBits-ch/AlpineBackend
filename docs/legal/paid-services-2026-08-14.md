## 2. What Is Sold

**2.1 Guild plans.** A paid plan applied to a single guild, raising that guild's capacity: voice room size, video and screenshare quality ceilings, upload size, storage, custom emoji slots, installed bots, audit log window, and the set of optional guild modules available. A guild plan is bought by a person and applied to a guild; it does not travel with the guild's members.

**2.2 Venta Plus.** A subscription attached to your own account, covering capabilities that travel with you across every guild you are in: larger uploads, higher-quality publishing where the guild's own plan permits it, additional registered devices, and profile customisation.

**2.3 Boosts.** A purchase made by a member and applied to a guild they belong to. Boosts accumulate against a guild and can unlock guild-plan capacity without the guild's owner buying a plan directly. A boost is applied at your direction and, once applied, is consumed for the period it covers.

**2.4 Add-ons.** Individually priced features, which may be charged on usage rather than on a fixed period. Where a feature is charged on usage, that is stated clearly before you enable it, along with how the usage is measured.

**2.5 Where a guild plan and Venta Plus both apply.** Some capabilities are decided by both sides. Where the guild's plan sets a ceiling on what the guild will distribute and your own subscription sets a ceiling on what you may publish, the lower of the two applies. Venta Plus does not raise a guild above its own plan, and a guild's plan does not raise you above yours.

**11.4** If you pay the outstanding amount after a downgrade, the plan is restored. A new billing period starts from the date payment succeeds.
